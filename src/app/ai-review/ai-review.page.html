How It Checks the Weight

  • Bar speed. Near a true max the bar moves slowly. A new best lifted fast suggests lighter plates than claimed.
  • Fatigue. In a hard set the reps slow down. Reps that stay quick to the end suggest a light load.
  • Progress. A gain far beyond what training usually gives in the time since the last verified result.
  • Body weight and balance. Lifts far past elite for the athlete's body weight, or out of line with their other lifts.
  • The plates. Stills from the start, middle and end of each video, and your quick "looks right" or "doubtful" label, which it learns from.
  • Fakes. Cuts in the video, and the same footage sent before by anyone.
